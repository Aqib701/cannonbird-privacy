This Privacy Policy explains how Cannon Bird (“the app”, “the game”), published on Google Play by AllOut Games (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information. We wrote it to be short and clear, because the game is simple: Cannon Bird does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive user data.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no personal data leaves your device. Your best distance, best score, coins, upgrades and sound settings are stored only on your own device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data. In particular, the app does not collect or ask for:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • account details or login credentials (the game has no accounts or sign-in);
  • precise or approximate location;
  • photos, videos, audio or microphone and camera data;
  • contacts, calendar, messages or files;
  • advertising IDs, device identifiers (such as IMEI or Android ID), or other persistent identifiers;
  • health, financial or payment information (the game has no purchases of any kind);
  • browsing history, app-usage analytics or crash-report data.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the game saves the following only on your own device, using Android’s standard app storage:

  • your best distance and best score;
  • the coins you have collected and the upgrade levels you bought with them in the in-game shop;
  • your sound effects and music settings (on/off and volume);
  • a small flag that remembers whether the first-flight tutorial has been shown.

This information never leaves your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and does not identify you. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Cannon Bird → Storage → Clear data).

3. How we use information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for advertising, profiling, analytics, personalization, or any other purpose. The locally saved game data in section 2 is used solely to run the game on your device.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any user data to any third party, because none is collected. The game contains no advertising networks, analytics services, social-media SDKs or other third-party services that collect user data.

Two things outside the app are worth knowing:

  • Google Play. If you install the game through Google Play, Google handles your download and installation under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal information from Google.
  • This web page. The game has a “Privacy Policy” button that opens this page in your browser. This page is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages, so the host (GitHub) may process technical data such as your IP address when you open it, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not receive or store that information.

5. Permissions

The game does not request any special Android permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, or storage access. The game plays fully offline and does not use the network for gameplay.

6. Data security

Since we collect no personal data and run no servers for the game, there is no personal data on our side to protect or breach. The data saved on your device stays inside the app’s private storage area, which Android protects from other apps. We recommend keeping your device’s operating system up to date and protected with a screen lock.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any user data, because we do not receive any. The only data related to you is the game progress saved on your device (section 2). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the game. There is no account to delete and no data held by us to request deletion of.

8. Children’s privacy

Cannon Bird is a general-audience arcade game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game collects no personal data and contains no ads or social features, there is no child data to collect, use or delete. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us at the email above and we will act on it promptly.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to its sale or sharing. Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, sell or share. If you have any privacy question or request, contact us and we will respond.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if we ever add a feature that collects data), we will update this page, change the “Last updated” date at the top, and update the game’s Google Play “Data safety” section before releasing the change.
